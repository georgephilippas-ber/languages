## la liquidité

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** The ability to meet obligations or sell an asset for cash.

**Grammar:** Noun, shown with its definite article.

**Example:** *La banque maintient un niveau élevé de liquidité.*

**English:** **liquidity** · **German:** **die Liquidität**

## le rendement

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Grammar:** Noun, shown with its definite article.

**Example:** *Le rendement de l'obligation a augmenté.*

**English:** **yield** · **German:** **die Rendite**
