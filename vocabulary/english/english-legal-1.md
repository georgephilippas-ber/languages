## to indemnify against

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** To compensate for a specified loss or protect against liability.

**Grammar:** Verb phrase; use the complement shown in the heading.

**Example:** *The supplier agreed to indemnify the buyer against third-party claims.*

**German:** **gegen Schäden freistellen** · **French:** **garantir contre les pertes**

## to accede to

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** To agree to a request or formally become a party to a treaty.

**Grammar:** Verb phrase; use the complement shown in the heading.

**Example:** *The state decided to accede to the convention.*

**German:** **einem Übereinkommen beitreten** · **French:** **adhérer à une convention**

## a breach of contract

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** A failure to perform an obligation under a contract.

**Grammar:** Countable noun phrase.

**Example:** *Late delivery may constitute a breach of contract.*

**German:** **die Vertragsverletzung** · **French:** **la violation du contrat**

## liability

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** Legal responsibility for an act, omission, or debt.

**Grammar:** Noun, used in the relevant professional context.

**Example:** *The clause limits the company's liability for indirect losses.*

**German:** **die Haftung** · **French:** **la responsabilité**

## an injunction

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** A court order requiring or forbidding a particular act.

**Grammar:** Countable noun phrase.

**Example:** *The court granted an injunction to prevent disclosure.*

**German:** **die einstweilige Verfügung** · **French:** **l'injonction**
