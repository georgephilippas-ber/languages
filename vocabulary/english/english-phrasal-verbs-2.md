## to pore over

**CEFR:** roughly **C1–C2**.

**Definition:** To study something with great attention.

**Synonym:** scrutinise.

**Grammar:** Transitive and inseparable: pore over a document.

**Example:** *Analysts pored over the accounts before recommending the acquisition.*

**German:** **etwas eingehend prüfen** · **French:** **éplucher**
