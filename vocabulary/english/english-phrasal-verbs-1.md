## to bear out

**CEFR:** roughly **C1**.

**Definition:** To support a claim or conclusion with evidence.

**Synonym:** corroborate.

**Grammar:** Separable transitive: bear something out; bear it out. The forms are bear, bore, borne.

**Example:** *The later data bore out her original hypothesis.*

**German:** **bestätigen / untermauern** · **French:** **confirmer / corroborer**

Useful nuance: Used for evidence or events that show a statement was justified.

## to boil down to

**CEFR:** roughly **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** To have something as the essential or most important point.

**Synonym:** reduce to.

**Grammar:** Transitive and inseparable: boil down to something.

**Verb:** *boil down to a disagreement*.

**Example:** *The dispute boils down to a disagreement about responsibility.*

**German:** **sich im Kern reduzieren auf** · **French:** **se résumer à**

Useful nuance: This is often informal even when used in analytical discussion.
