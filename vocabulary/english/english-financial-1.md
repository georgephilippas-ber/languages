## liquidity

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** The ability to meet obligations or convert an asset into cash.

**Grammar:** Noun, used in the relevant professional context.

**Example:** *The bank holds liquid assets to maintain liquidity.*

**German:** **die Liquidität** · **French:** **la liquidité**

## yield

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Grammar:** Noun, used in the relevant professional context.

**Example:** *The bond's yield rose when its price fell.*

**German:** **die Rendite** · **French:** **le rendement**

## leverage

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Grammar:** Noun, used in the relevant professional context.

**Example:** *Higher leverage magnifies both potential gains and losses.*

## to hedge against

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** To reduce exposure to an unwanted financial risk.

**Grammar:** Verb phrase; use the complement shown in the heading.

**Example:** *The exporter hedged against currency fluctuations.*

**German:** **sich gegen Risiken absichern** · **French:** **se couvrir contre un risque**
