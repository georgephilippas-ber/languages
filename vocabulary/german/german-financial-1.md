## die Liquidität

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** The ability to meet payment obligations as they fall due.

**Grammar:** Feminine noun, with its definite article.

**Example:** *Das Unternehmen sicherte seine Liquidität durch eine Kreditlinie.*

**English:** **liquidity** · **French:** **la liquidité**

## die Rendite

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** The return on an investment relative to its value.

**Grammar:** Feminine noun, with its definite article.

**Example:** *Die Rendite der Anleihe ist gestiegen.*

**English:** **yield** · **French:** **le rendement**

## die Bilanz

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** A statement of assets, liabilities, and equity at a date.

**Grammar:** Feminine noun, with its definite article.

**Example:** *Die Bilanz weist hohe liquide Mittel aus.*

**English:** **balance sheet** · **French:** **le bilan**

## die Verschuldung

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** The amount or state of being in debt.

**Grammar:** Feminine noun, with its definite article.

**Example:** *Die Verschuldung des Konzerns nahm ab.*

**English:** **indebtedness** · **French:** **l'endettement**

## der Zinssatz

**CEFR:** approximately **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** The percentage rate charged or paid as interest.

**Grammar:** Masculine noun, with its definite article.

**Example:** *Der Zinssatz des Kredits ist variabel.*

**English:** **interest rate** · **French:** **le taux d'intérêt**
