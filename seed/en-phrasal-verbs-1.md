## to get up

**CEFR:** roughly **A2**.

**Definition:** To leave your bed after sleeping.

**Synonym:** rise.

**Grammar:** Intransitive: get up; got up; got up.

**Example:** *I get up early when I have a morning class.*

**German:** **aufstehen** · **French:** **se lever**

Useful nuance: Unlike wake up, get up emphasizes leaving bed.

## to wake up

**CEFR:** roughly **A2**.

**Definition:** To stop sleeping or cause someone to stop sleeping.

**Synonym:** awaken.

**Grammar:** Intransitive or separable transitive: wake up; woke up; woken up. A pronoun object goes between the verb
and up.

**Example:** *The noise woke her up before sunrise.*

**German:** **aufwachen / wecken** · **French:** **se réveiller / réveiller quelqu’un**

Useful nuance: Waking up does not necessarily mean getting out of bed.

## to put on

**Definition:** To dress yourself in a piece of clothing or an accessory.

**Synonym:** dress in.

**German:** **anziehen** · **French:** **mettre un vêtement**

Useful nuance: This sense concerns clothing; put on can also mean to arrange a performance.
