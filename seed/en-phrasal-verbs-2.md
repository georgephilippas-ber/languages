## to account for

**CEFR:** roughly **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** To explain why something happened or why it has a particular value.

**Synonym:** explain.

**Grammar:** Transitive and inseparable: account for something.

**Verb:** *account for a result*.

**Example:** *The model does not account for the difference in prices.*

**German:** **erklären / begründen** · **French:** **expliquer**

Useful nuance: Account for can also mean constitute a proportion of a total.

## to amount to

**CEFR:** roughly **B2–C1**.

**Definition:** To be equivalent in effect or meaning to something.

**Synonym:** be equivalent to.

**Grammar:** Transitive and inseparable: amount to something.

**Verb:** *amount to a ban*.

Useful nuance: It can also mean add up to a numerical total.

## to bear out

Useful nuance: Used for evidence or events that show a statement was justified.
