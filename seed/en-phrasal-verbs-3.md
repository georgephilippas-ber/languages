## to abide by

**CEFR:** roughly **C1–C2**.

**Definition:** To comply with a rule or decision.

**Synonym:** comply with.

**Grammar:** Transitive and inseparable: abide by a rule.

**Example:** *The contractor must abide by the tribunal’s final decision.*

**German:** **sich an etwas halten** · **French:** **respecter**
